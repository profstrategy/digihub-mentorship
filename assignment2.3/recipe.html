How to Prepare Nigerian Party Jollof Rice

Nigerian Party Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, and a blend of spices. It is commonly served at parties, family gatherings, and special occasions. This recipe explains how to prepare delicious Jollof Rice at home.

Ingredients

Here are the ingredients needed to prepare the meal:

  • 3 cups of long-grain parboiled rice
  • 5 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 2 medium-sized onions
  • Half a cup of vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of thyme
  • Salt to taste
  • Chicken stock or water

Preparation Steps

Follow these steps to prepare Nigerian Jollof Rice:

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly with clean water and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, and one onion until smooth.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot and add the chopped onion and tomato paste. Fry for a few minutes.
  4. Pour in the blended pepper mixture and cook until the mixture thickens and some of its moisture evaporates.
  5. Add the seasoning cubes, curry powder, thyme, and salt. Stir properly to combine the ingredients.
  6. Add the chicken stock or water and bring the mixture to a boil.
  7. Add the washed rice and stir until it is evenly distributed in the sauce.
  8. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed.
  9. Stir gently, check that the rice is fully cooked, and allow it to steam for a few more minutes if necessary.
  10. Serve hot with fried plantain, chicken, or your preferred side dish.

Important Safety Notes

Handle hot oil carefully: Avoid adding wet ingredients carelessly to hot oil because it can splatter and cause burns.

Use clean cooking equipment: Wash your hands, utensils, and cooking surfaces before preparing the ingredients.

Important: Keep the cooking pot covered when appropriate, but monitor the heat to prevent the rice from burning.

Common Cooking Terms

Parboiling
Partially boiling rice before completing the cooking process. Parboiled rice is processed before sale and is commonly used for Jollof Rice.
Sautéing
Cooking ingredients such as onions in a small amount of hot oil for a short period.
Simmering
Cooking food gently in liquid at a temperature just below boiling.
Steaming
Cooking food using the heat from trapped steam, usually inside a covered pot.
